Government Exam Image Guide

Government Exam Photo & Signature Requirements

Always check the latest official examination notification before submitting your application.

SSC Application Photo & Signature

SSC application portals may require candidates to upload photographs, signatures and documents according to specifications mentioned in the particular examination notification. File size, format and dimensions can vary between examinations.

UPSC Application Images

UPSC application systems can require photographs, signatures and identity documents with specific technical requirements. Candidates should always follow the instructions in the current official notification.

Banking Exam Photo & Signature

Banking recruitment applications commonly require a photograph and signature in specified file formats, dimensions and file-size ranges. Requirements can differ between recruitment examinations.

Railway & Other Government Forms

Railway, state government and other recruitment portals may use different image-upload rules. Always compare the final file with the requirements listed on the official application portal.

⚠️ Important

There is no universal government-form photo size. Requirements can change by examination and year. Always verify the current official notification before uploading your image.
